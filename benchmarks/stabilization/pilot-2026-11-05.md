| Melies stage | Unmodified | 99.4798 | 100 | 100 | 100 |
| Melies stage | Synthetic jitter | 99.3304 | 100 | 100 | 100 |

For these short conditions, the source-area crop estimator at zooms 1/2/3%
reported **1.99 / 3.96 / 5.91%**, respectively. This is raster support of the
stabilizer's input, not continuous area, interpolation-kernel support or proof
of image content: existing black borders and borders introduced by synthetic
input generation are not detected as missing content by the white-canvas replay.

No cuts were automatically detected in these short windows. Cuts were not
human annotated, so there is no measured cut-detection accuracy. The adjacent
motion diagnostic accepted all 23 pairs per rendered condition; acceptance is
not proof of correct historical-camera-motion recovery.

**Do not infer that 1% zoom is a safe default.** These are approximately
one-second windows, not evidence about pans, cut transitions or long-shot drift.
No visual winner, stronger-motion preset or historical-fidelity claim follows.
